Codinome...

One may ask why it would take me so long to let go
I say that it is too much to forget
For I once knew you from inside out
For I once could tell by the cold air winding through my window
Which movie you'd be watching on a cloudy Sunday afternoon
For I remember all the poems we wrote to the moon
For I once could tell your thoughts by the way you'd say "hi"
For I was there at low and high
For I knew everything
Your favorite breakfast,lunch and dinner
Nnames for your children
Let them be boy,girl,blond or brunnete
I knew how to make you feel safe, only two fingers needed
I knew all your fears, all the bad memories and the insecurities you created
And I even stayed enough to take some of them away
I knew your dream's dress, the song at the church's entrance
I remember our dances, your low head at my fast beating chest
I remember our days as we had lunch and talked by the glass
I remember our "accident"
I learned all the names from your family, and their stories
I learned about your grandfather, your mother
The fight about your grandmother and the guitar
I'd know when,why and how you'd come to me when you needed
For I knew not your colour, but your whole favorite pallets
For I remember when we first held hands
For I remember when we last felt like friends
For I was once able to read you like an open and transparent book
And for how big you are
I'd say
I'm actually being pretty fast at forgetting
Fly well, autumm butterfly
